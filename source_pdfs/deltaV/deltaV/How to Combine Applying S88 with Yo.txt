How to Combine Applying S88 with Your Two DeltaV Manuals
Since Applying S88 is only ~155 pages long, read it first before diving deep into the 616-page Course 7016 manual. The three documents fit together in a specific, logical sequence:

First: Read Chapters 3, 4, and 5 of Applying S88 (The Architectural "Why")

The "Collapsible Model" Trap: Parshall & Lamb explain the single biggest architectural debate in DCS design: Should your Unit Phases talk to Equipment Modules (EMs), or talk directly to Control Modules (CMs)? They show the pros and cons of both, and why packing too much recipe logic into an Equipment Module ruins your flexibility, while making Phases too granular turns your recipes into a nightmare.

Exception Handling Philosophy: They break down the real-world rules of HOLD, STOP, and ABORT—specifically how to classify equipment states so a failed valve on an inlet manifold safely pauses the active phase without dumping the whole batch.
